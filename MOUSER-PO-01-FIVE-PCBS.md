## Outside this Mouser PO

Supply 5 hiBCTR RP2040-Zero modules in total (reuse any already owned) and 5 fabricated Rev D PCBs. Module: Amazon ASIN B0DXL12W59. PCB fabrication files are in hardware/rev-d/. No enclosure has been specified; this order builds open boards on insulating feet.

## Module sockets: specific orientation

Each PCB uses two SSW-109-01-T-S side sockets and FIVE SSW-101-01-T-S single-contact sockets at M1 contacts 10-14. Turn each single socket so its 3.05 mm housing dimension runs parallel to the long side sockets and its 2.41 mm dimension runs along the five-contact end row. Use the module to align them before soldering. A complete SSW-105 strip has end overhang that nominally overlaps the side sockets at the corners; it is deliberately not on this PO. Sample-fit the actual module and sockets before assembly.

## Header pack allocation

One Adafruit 392 pack contains ten 36-pin strips. Cut one strip per board into 9 + 9 + 5 male module contacts and a 2-pin JP1 header. Five strips cover all five units (125 contacts used); five strips remain spare. Do not order five packs.

## Fuse clips

Order 10 individual 01110501Z clips for five fuse positions, plus the five 1.5 A fast 5x20 mm fuses. Fit each pair with retaining ears toward the outer ends of the fuse. The clips match the footprint family identified in the saved PCB. No inline holders are needed.

## Power and custom cables

Use one regulated 5 V supply and one barrel adapter per unit. Connect adapter + to PCB J1.1 and - to J1.2; the PCB fuse is already in series. Use ten P222-006 cables because each has only one male PS/2 end. Cut about 3 ft from each male end and solder one SDR-70 plug using the Rev D cable table. Continuity-test contacts; do not assume wire colors. Use the plug clamps for strain relief.

## Shared supplies and tools

Wire, heat-shrink, header pack, programming cable and multimeter are shared across all five builds. Soldering iron, electronics solder, cutters/stripper, screwdriver and heat source are existing workshop supplies, not included purchase lines. Firmware testing also needs a computer and suitable test equipment. Existing keyboard/mouse receivers and X16 are not part of this order.

## Test access and prototype status

TP1-TP26 are bare plated PCB holes, so no separate test-point parts are required. This purchase list does not establish physical fit or a working product: Rev D remains an engineering prototype, with firmware and bench validation pending.
